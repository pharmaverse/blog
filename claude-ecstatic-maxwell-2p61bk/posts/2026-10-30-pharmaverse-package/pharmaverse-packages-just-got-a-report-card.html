Pharmaverse Packages Just Got a Report Card

Every pharmaverse-endorsed package now carries lifecycle tags and quality badges, reviewed twice a year by the council: proactive support for maintainers and an early signal for users.

NoteTL;DR

Every pharmaverse-endorsed package now carries a set of tags showing its lifecycle status and quality. Maintainers get proactive support and clear expectations. Users get an early signal when a package they rely on needs a closer look. Reviewed twice a year. Full model linked below.

Why we’re doing this

Pharmaverse’s name carries weight — when we endorse a package, people build submissions on it. That’s a responsibility we take seriously, and until now it’s been an implicit one. There was no consistent way to tell whether a package was thriving, quietly stalling, or overdue for a second look.

This model makes that visible, on a predictable schedule, with a process designed to support maintainer teams rather than blindside them.

What’s new for maintainers

  • You’ll be notified directly before any status change is made public — Watch, At Risk, or otherwise. No surprises.
  • The council now owes you something too. Early outreach when activity dips, help finding a co-steward if you need to step back, and support pulling together a remediation plan if one’s ever needed.
  • A 30-day appeal window on any Watch or At Risk call.
  • Clear, published criteria for what “quality reviewed” and “submission-suitable” actually mean — see the glossary below.

What’s new for users

  • Tags are visible on every package page — a 10-second read tells you whether a package is safe to build on, newly emerging, or showing signs of drift.
  • “Drift” won’t stay invisible. If a package you depend on starts sliding — maintainer gone quiet, quality regressing — you’ll see it before it becomes a problem in your workflow.

How often does this happen?

Twice a year. Reviews are lightweight by design — packages with nothing to flag are reconfirmed by consent in minutes. The council only spends time where something’s actually changed.

The tags, at a glance

Tag Meaning User signal
Stable Actively maintained, API settled. Quality badges fully active. Safe to adopt
Maturing Under active development. Filling a genuine gap. Quality badges not yet assessed. Assess fit carefully
Watch Concerns raised by the council. Quality badges under scrutiny. Use with caution
At Risk Failing to meet pharmaverse standards. Remediation plan required within one quarter. Avoid for new work
Archived Endorsement withdrawn. Package retained for reference only. No quality badges displayed. Do not use

Submission Readiness

Badge Signals
✅ Submission-Suitable* Lower validation burden for sponsors
⚠ Submission-Caution Extra sponsor-side validation needed

Maintenance Health

Badge Signals
🔧 Actively Maintained Responsive team, regular releases
💤 Low Maintenance Slowing down — worth watching

Technical Quality

Badge Signals
🔬 Quality Reviewed Passed formal pharmaverse review
📋 Review Pending Not yet reviewed — not a red flag

* Submission-Suitable doesn’t replace your own validation — see the full model for the disclaimer.

Got questions?

If your package is up for its first review, you’ll hear from us directly well before anything changes.
